Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Who we are

Serrano Studio Photography (“the Studio,” “we,” “us”) is the photography business of Juan Serrano, serving Southern California. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through juanserranostudio.com and when you contact or book us. Questions: info@juanserranostudio.com or (213) 904-3004.

What we collect, and why

Quote requests. When you send the quote form we receive your name, email, phone (optional), the service you’re interested in, event date, location and any details you write. We use this only to answer your request, check availability and prepare a quote.

Direct contact. If you email, call, text, or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, we receive whatever you choose to share and use it to respond.

Bookings. If you book, we keep the details needed to deliver the service — names, contact details, event schedule and venue, your signed agreement, and payment records. Card payments, if any, are handled by the payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

Your photographs. Images we take at your session or event are personal information about the people in them; see “Photographs and image use” below.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don’t build marketing profiles.

Legal basis and consent

We process quote requests on the basis of your consent — the form cannot be sent until you tick the consent box — and because you asked us to take steps before entering into an agreement. Booking information is processed to perform our agreement with you and to meet legal obligations such as tax records. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the Studio, and only as needed:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) relays quote-form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP address for security.
  • Google Fonts serves the site’s typefaces; your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address. No cookies are set.
  • Gallery, payment and contract tools we may use for booked clients, under their own privacy terms.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Photographs and image use

Photographs are taken under your booking agreement. We use images in our portfolio, website or social media only as your agreement allows. You can ask us at any time to stop using an image of you (or your child) for promotion, and we will remove it from the pages we control. Images of minors — for example at quinceañeras or youth sports — are taken and used only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

Retention

Quote requests that don’t lead to a booking are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted. Booking and payment records are kept as long as needed to fulfill the agreement and as required by tax and business law (generally up to 7 years). Archived images are kept as described in your agreement.

Security

Access to your information is limited to the Studio, and we use reasonable safeguards such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them through the site. Quotes and bookings involving minors must be made by a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to know how it is used. Email info@juanserranostudio.com; we will verify the request and respond within 45 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We honor Global Privacy Control signals where required; since we don’t sell or share data, there is nothing further for a signal to switch off.

California residents

Where the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) applies, California residents have the rights above, including to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing — and we do not sell or share personal information. Under California’s “Shine the Light” law you may request information about disclosures for direct marketing; we make none. In line with CalOPPA, this site does not track visitors across other sites, so browser “Do Not Track” signals do not change how it behaves.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. The effective date below shows the current version; material changes will be noted on this page.
