Refund Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Your signed booking agreement sets the exact amounts and deadlines for your event. This page summarizes how they generally work; if it differs from your agreement, the agreement controls.

Pricing

Prices aren’t posted online. Every quinceañera, wedding, photo booth, photoshoot or sports booking is quoted individually after we review your date, location and coverage.

Retainer

A retainer reserves your date and is applied toward your total. Because we turn away other clients for that date, the retainer is generally non-refundable, except where the Studio cancels or your agreement says otherwise.

If you need to cancel

Tell us in writing as early as possible. Any refund of amounts paid beyond the retainer depends on how far in advance you cancel, as set out in your agreement.

Rescheduling

We’ll do our best to move your session or event to a new date, subject to availability. Payments made are normally transferred to the new date.

If the Studio has to cancel

If we can’t provide the service for reasons on our side, you may choose to rebook or receive a full refund of payments for services not delivered. If Juan is unable to attend due to an emergency, we will first try to arrange a qualified replacement photographer with your approval.

Weather and events beyond anyone’s control

For outdoor sessions affected by weather, we’ll reschedule at no extra charge. Cancellations caused by events beyond either party’s control (such as natural disasters or public-health orders) are handled as described in your agreement.

Delivered work

Once images or products have been delivered, they are not refundable. If something isn’t right, contact us — we want you to love your photos.
