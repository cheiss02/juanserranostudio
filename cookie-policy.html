Cookie Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This site does not use cookies, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other tracking technology.

What we store on your device

The site keeps two small items in your browser’s local storage (not cookies). They stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • ss_lang — your language choice (English or Spanish), so the site remembers it.
  • ss_consent — whether you closed the privacy notice and which option you chose.

If you choose Essential only in the notice, the site stops remembering your language between visits and stores only that choice.

Third parties

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address but sets no cookies. The quote form contacts FormSubmit only when you press send. Links to Instagram or WhatsApp open those services, which have their own cookie policies.

Managing storage

You can clear local storage at any time in your browser settings (usually under “Site data” or “Cookies and site data”). The site will work normally afterwards.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential tracking, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent with equally visible Accept and Reject options before anything is set.
