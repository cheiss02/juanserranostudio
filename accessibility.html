Accessibility

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to see Juan’s work and get in touch. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA on every page, in English and Spanish.

What we’ve done

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • Photos have text descriptions, and gallery captions are real text.
  • Text can be resized to 200% and the layout adapts down to small phones.
  • Buttons and links have large touch targets.
  • Animations are reduced or turned off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • The whole site is available in English and Spanish.

Known limitations

The rotating photo sphere on the home page is a visual, drag-based experience. The same photos are available as a simple grid (the grid button on the home page) and on the Sport gallery page, which work fully with keyboards and screen readers.

Reporting a problem

If something is hard to use, please tell us at info@juanserranostudio.com or (213) 904-3004. Include the page and, if you can, your browser or assistive technology. We’ll reply and work on a fix, and we can always share information in another format.
